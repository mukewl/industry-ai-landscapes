# Gaming — disruption frame

**Status: draft — review with user before population begins.**

## The question
Who can use AI/agentic technology to disrupt **game discovery & distribution** — the storefront take-rate model?

## Incumbent under threat
Steam (Valve), the console stores (PlayStation / Xbox / Nintendo), and the mobile app stores (Apple / Google): the ~30%-take rails between studios and players.
**Incumbent benchmark** (vs-incumbent bars + quadrant dot; the `AMA` constant when the dashboard is adapted): `{w: 30, d: 95, ai: 40}` — little appetite to disrupt its own economics, near-total distribution power, middling consumer AI.

## The three pillars (full no-storefront stack = all three)
| Pillar | Meaning | Skeleton mapping |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience** (demand) | Owns player reach that would follow it to a new channel | `w5 ≥ 4` |
| **Catalog** (content) | Owns/aggregates games & IP players want | `d1 ≥ 4` or `direct_channel = y` |
| **Rails** (settlement) | Store-grade payments/MoR + delivery infrastructure | `merchant_of_record = y` or `d4 ≥ 4` |

## Vocabulary
- **Value chain (VC1–VC7)**: Discover → Evaluate → Acquire → Pay → Play → Engage → Retain
- **Verticals (Phase 2)**: PC · Console · Mobile · Cloud streaming · UGC platforms
- **Sector taxonomy** (star colors, ~counts to aim for in a 130-company set):
  Game-native (studios/publishers/launchers) `#ff6b81` ~45 · Storefront & platform `#ffb74d` ~15 · AI-native gaming `#60a5fa` ~20 · Big Tech platform `#b388ff` ~12 · Fintech & payments `#4ade80` ~12 · Media & community `#22d3ee` ~16 · Other `#9fb0cc` ~10
- **Direct channel** = own launcher/store/web-shop bypassing platform storefronts (post-*Epic v. Apple* external checkout counts).

## Why this frame is live right now
Epic v. Apple/Google rulings opening external checkout; web-shops (Supercell, Scopely) bypassing app stores; Discord/YouTube/TikTok as the real discovery layer; AI NPC/UGC platforms creating content outside publisher pipelines; agentic assistants that could recommend-and-buy games directly.

## Candidate scenario seeds (for the AI simulator later)
"Discord launches a game store" · "Epic wins external checkout everywhere" · "Netflix acquires a major publisher" · "AI agents buy games for players based on playstyle".
