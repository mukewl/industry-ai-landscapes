# Luxury — disruption frame

**Status: draft — review with user before population begins.**

## The question
Who can use AI to break the **controlled-distribution maison model** — the deliberate scarcity, price discipline and one-to-one client ownership that luxury houses enforce through boutiques and tightly-policed wholesale?

## Incumbent under threat
The maisons and their distribution discipline: LVMH, Kering, Richemont, Hermès, Chanel — houses whose power rests on controlling *where* product appears, *what it costs*, and *who owns the client relationship*.
**Incumbent benchmark**: `{w: 25, d: 90, ai: 35}` — actively hostile to disrupting their own model, formidable controlled distribution, cautious AI adoption (clienteling pilots, not agents).

## The three pillars (full maison-bypass stack = all three)
| Pillar | Meaning | Skeleton mapping |
|---|---|---|
| **Clientele** (demand) | Owns affluent clients + their data/preferences | `w5 ≥ 4` |
| **Maison** (content) | Access to genuine coveted supply — brands, consignment, or own houses | `d1 ≥ 4` or `direct_channel = y` |
| **Experience** (settlement) | Luxury-grade transaction + service + aftercare rails | `merchant_of_record = y` or `d4 ≥ 4` |

## Vocabulary
- **Value chain (VC1–VC7)**: Inspire → Discover → Consider → Purchase → Own → Aftercare → Resale
- **Verticals (Phase 2)**: Fashion & leather · Watches & jewelry · Beauty & fragrance · Wines & spirits · Hospitality & experiences
- **Sector taxonomy**:
  Maison & house `#ff6b81` ~25 · Luxury retail & e-tail `#ffb74d` ~22 · Resale & authentication `#22d3ee` ~20 · AI-native `#60a5fa` ~18 · Big Tech platform `#b388ff` ~10 · Fintech & payments `#4ade80` ~12 · Other `#9fb0cc` ~13
- **Direct channel** = a client-owned route (AI stylist, resale platform, social commerce) outside maison-controlled distribution.

## Why this frame is live right now
Resale (Vestiaire, The RealReal, Watchfinder) created liquid secondary markets houses don't control; AI authentication (Entrupy) removes the trust moat; AI personal shoppers/stylists could own the client conversation; livestream/social selling (esp. China) reroutes discovery; e-tailer consolidation (Farfetch/YNAP turmoil) shows the old digital-wholesale model breaking — leaving a vacuum something agentic could fill.

## Candidate scenario seeds
"An AI personal shopper owns the top 1% client relationship" · "Vestiaire + authentication AI beats the boutique on trust" · "Amazon cracks luxury via Saks partnership" · "A maison launches its own resale + digital-ID rail".

APAC: Tmall Luxury, Douyin, WeChat), payments/BNPL-for-luxury, digital ID/blockchain (Aura), Big Tech.
