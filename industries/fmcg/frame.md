# FMCG — disruption frame

**Status: draft — review with user before population begins.**

## The question
Who can use **agentic commerce** to bypass the **retail shelf** — the brand × big-retail model where CPG giants buy shelf/search placement and the retailer owns the shopper?

## Incumbent under threat
The shelf itself, jointly held: CPG giants (P&G, Unilever, Nestlé, Coca-Cola…) × the retail gatekeepers (Walmart, Tesco, Carrefour, Amazon) and their retail-media towers.
**Incumbent benchmark**: `{w: 35, d: 95, ai: 45}` — incremental DTC appetite, unmatched physical distribution, serious but shelf-serving AI (retail media, demand forecasting).

## The three pillars (full shelf-bypass stack = all three)
| Pillar | Meaning | Skeleton mapping |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | Owns a direct, repeat consumer relationship (app/subscription/assistant) | `w5 ≥ 4` |
| **Product** | Owns or aggregates the brands/assortment for the basket | `d1 ≥ 4` or `direct_channel = y` |
| **Fulfilment** | Owns checkout + last-mile/replenishment billing | `merchant_of_record = y` or `d4 ≥ 4` |

## Vocabulary
- **Value chain (VC1–VC7)**: Awareness → Consideration → Purchase → Fulfilment → Consumption → Replenishment → Loyalty
- **Verticals (Phase 2)**: Food & beverage · Home care · Personal care & beauty · Health & wellness · Pet
- **Sector taxonomy**:
  CPG brand `#ff6b81` ~30 · Retail & commerce platform `#ffb74d` ~25 · AI-native commerce `#60a5fa` ~20 · Big Tech platform `#b388ff` ~12 · Fintech & payments `#4ade80` ~15 · DTC & challenger brands `#22d3ee` ~18 · Other `#9fb0cc` ~10
- **Direct channel** = DTC/subscription/agent-checkout route that skips the shelf entirely.

## Why this frame is live right now
Agentic checkout (ChatGPT shopping, Perplexity Buy, Amazon Rufus/Buy-for-me) threatens both brand search *and* retail media; replenishment subscriptions turn one purchase into a locked-in stream; quick-commerce compresses fulfilment; retail media ~$150B+ is the profit pool a bypass would strand; private label + AI formulation attack the brand moat itself.

## Candidate scenario seeds
"ChatGPT becomes the default grocery list" · "Amazon lets agents reorder the whole basket" · "Instacart partners with OpenAI" · "P&G goes subscription-direct for its top 20 brands".

## Seed-list starting points (Phase C research)
CPG majors + challengers, retailers/quick-commerce (Walmart, Instacart, Getir-likes, Flink…), agentic commerce (OpenAI, Perplexity, Amazon Rufus…), checkout/payments (Stripe, Shopify, Bolt…), replenishment/subscription DTC, retail-media/adtech, supply-chain AI (Keychain, Crisp…), Big Tech.
