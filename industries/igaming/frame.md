# iGaming — disruption frame

**Status: draft — review with user before population begins.**

## The question
Who can use AI to **own the player relationship** — and bypass the licensed operator that owns it today?

## Incumbent under threat
The **licensed multi-brand operator + its affiliate-SEO acquisition rail**: Betsson, Entain, Flutter, 888/evoke, Kindred et al., who rent player intent from Better Collective / Catena / Gambling.com and Google, then own the account, wallet and lifetime value.
**Incumbent benchmark** (vs-incumbent bars + quadrant dot; the `AMA` constant when the dashboard is adapted): `{w: 35, d: 90, ai: 40}` — limited appetite to disrupt its own funnel, formidable licensed distribution (brands, licences, payment rails), competent-but-defensive AI (CRM, bonusing, risk).

## The three pillars (full operator-bypass stack = all three)
| Pillar | Meaning | Skeleton mapping |
|---|---|---|
| **Player** (demand) | Owns the intent moment — traffic it controls rather than rents | `w5 ≥ 4` |
| **Product** (content) | Owns odds/markets/games players actually want | `d1 ≥ 4` or `direct_channel = y` |
| **Wallet** (settlement) | Licensed money in and out — deposits, MoR, payouts | `merchant_of_record = y` or `d4 ≥ 4` |

A company holding all three can run a betting relationship end-to-end without a traditional operator.

## Why this frame is live right now
- **AI search is eating affiliate SEO** — the acquisition rail operators depend on is being disintermediated by answer engines; affiliate share prices and traffic have already re-rated.
- **Prediction markets** (Polymarket, Kalshi, Sporttrade) offer exchange-style event trading under a different regulatory theory — attacking sportsbook margin *and* the licence moat at once.
- **Agentic assistants** could compare odds, place and manage bets across books, turning operators into interchangeable liquidity.
- **The supply rail concentrates**: Evolution in live casino, Sportradar/Genius in data — a "rail beneath the rails" pattern (as in travel) where a few B2B firms power every brand.
- **Crypto/offshore books** (Stake, Rollbit) grow without local licences, proving players will follow product over jurisdiction.
- **Retention is now the battleground**: AI personalisation and responsible-gambling regulation pull in opposite directions on the same data.
