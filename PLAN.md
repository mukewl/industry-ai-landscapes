# PLAN.md — Industry AI Landscapes: the full roadmap

**Goal:** a job-application portfolio piece — three AI-disruption landscape dashboards (**gaming · FMCG · luxury**) on one public site with an industry toggle, each backed by a ~130-company, 111-column Excel built with the travel project's proven methodology and populated by research agents over multiple sessions.

**Provenance:** methodology and machinery come from the travel/Amadeus landscape project via its sanitized `template/` (this repo carries **zero client material**). Reference guides: `template/README.md` (architecture + deployment), `template/DATA-CONTRACT.md` (schema + scoring), `template/ADAPTATION-CHECKLIST.md` (29 adaptation steps).

## Locked decisions (see DECISIONS.md)

1. **Frames** — Gaming: AI/agentic disruption of game discovery & distribution (incumbents: Steam/app stores). FMCG: agentic commerce bypassing the retail shelf (incumbents: brand × big-retail). Luxury: AI vs the controlled-distribution maison model (incumbents: LVMH/Kering-style houses). Details per `industries/<ind>/frame.md`.
2. **Toggle** — one repo + one Vercel project; `/gaming` `/fmcg` `/luxury` as separate self-contained dashboards; header toggle navigates.
3. **Scope** — ~120–150 companies per industry, full column depth, evidence-cited.
4. **Home** — public repo `industry-ai-landscapes`, no Amadeus data.

## The shared schema (built — session 01)

One identical 111-column skeleton for all industries (travel had 112 — same depth), generated by `scripts/make_workbook.py`:
**PROFILE** (33: identity, funding, business model, MoR, legacy-rail/API/direct-channel flags, agentic protocols MCP/A2A/UCP/ACP, 11-field AI capability stack, VC1–VC7 value-chain coverage) · **POSITION** (15: 5 industry verticals × distributing+role, position class, motion, targets, rationale) · **POTENTIAL** (25: W1–W5, D1–D6, AI1–AI7 at 0–5 + 7 computed) · **POSTURE** (11: tier, horizon, impact-on-incumbent, actions, reviewer notes) · **ELIMINATION** (15: financials F1–F5, survival) · **EVIDENCE** (5: links, notes, confidence, researched on/by — new vs travel; what makes web-researched scores defensible).
Scoring model (Model Config sheet, same as travel): `EPI = .4·W + .6·R; R = .5·D + .5·AI`; signal weights W `.25/.25/.20/.20/.10`, D `.25/.25/.15/.15/.10/.10`, AI `.20/.25/.15/.10/.10/.15/.05`; bands ≥60 High / ≥40 Medium; quadrants (W≥60)×(R≥60). Derived scores computed **in Python** (populate.py), never Excel formulas.
Per-industry meaning lives in each workbook's **Scoring Guide** sheet (signal definitions + 0/3/5 anchors) and `frame.md`.

## Population methodology (every industry, every session)

1. **Seed** — agents sweep each taxonomy sector for candidates → dedupe/alias-check → ranked `seedlist.md` (~150; the extra ~20 covers attrition/dupes).
2. **Batches** — ~15–25 companies per batch: web research per company → all signals 0–5 scored against the Scoring Guide anchors → evidence links + one-line justifications → `scripts/populate.py` writes rows and computes W%/D%/AI%/EPI/tier/quadrant.
3. **Calibration** — batch 1 fixes ~5 anchor companies spanning the range; every ~50 companies re-check a sample against them; drift → rescore batch, log in DECISIONS.md.
4. **Bookkeeping** — PROGRESS.md counts + session log after every batch.

## ⏸ Phases C–E are DEFERRED (decision D10, 2026-07-13)

**We finish iGaming end-to-end first** — populated, analysed, dashboard built and deployed — and only then decide whether to do a second industry at all. Reasons: token cost is the binding constraint (~470k per 15-company batch before optimisation), and one deep, finished, deployed landscape is a stronger portfolio piece than four half-populated ones.

The machinery is industry-agnostic and stays ready: `gaming`, `fmcg` and `luxury` already have skeleton workbooks and draft frames, and `ORCHESTRATION.md` § "Adding a new industry" is a 4-step recipe. Nothing is lost by waiting; the toggle simply ships with one industry (or is hidden) until a second one exists.

## Risks / honest notes

- **Scores are web-researched estimates** — the Evidence columns + Scoring Guide anchors are the defense. Say so on the landing page; it reads as rigor, not weakness.
- Population is the long pole (~390 companies × deep research). Batch discipline + calibration prevent the classic multi-session drift.
- Gemini free tier trains on inputs; all data here is public-web-derived, fine. Keep the passcode on anyway.
- The first dashboard adaptation (B5) is the riskiest session — budget a full session; the checklist de-risks it.
